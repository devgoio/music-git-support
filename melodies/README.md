# Melodies Support

## Import your music

Tap + in Library and choose audio files from Files. To bring artwork and lyrics along, import a Melodies package or select the matching .mi file and its original audio. Melodies matches details to the audio content, including after a rename.

## Transfer between devices

Open Transfer on the receiving phone. Enable receiving and approve the connection. For browser uploads, keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi and enter the address shown on the phone.

## Choose the display language

Open Settings → Appearance → Language. Select System Default, 简体中文, or English. Your original track titles and lyrics stay in their original language.

## Contact support

Email [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn) with your app version and the steps where you need help.

# Melodies 技术支持

## 导入音乐

点曲库右上角的加号，从「文件」选择音频。如果要一起导入封面和歌词，可选取音乐包，或同时选取原始音频和对应的 .mi 资料文件。音频改名后仍可按内容匹配资料。

## 在设备之间传歌

在接收设备打开「传输」，开启接收并确认连接。电脑上传时，将两台设备接入同一 Wi-Fi，在浏览器输入手机显示的地址。

## 更换界面语言

打开「设置 → 外观 → 界面语言」，选择跟随系统、简体中文或 English。歌曲标题与歌词保留原文。

## 联系技术支持

请将应用版本和遇到问题的操作步骤发到 [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn)。
