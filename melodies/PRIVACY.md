# Melodies Privacy Policy

## Effective November 23, 2026

Dev Go provides Melodies. Core playback and library management work offline without an account. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

## On your device

Imported audio, artwork, lyrics, playlists, listening history, and preferences are stored on your device. You can delete imported tracks and artwork in the app. Optional headphone controls process motion locally while enabled.

## Transfers you start

Nearby transfer sends your selections directly to the recipient you choose. Browser uploads require approval on your phone and use your local network. When you share via another app, that service handles the shared files under its own privacy policy.

## Optional AI tools

If you configure a provider and start a lookup, Melodies sends the selected provider your track filename, title, artist, album, duration, a lyric excerpt and any hints you enter. OpenAI, Kimi or DeepSeek process these requests using your own API key. Artwork generation sends track details to OpenAI. Audio clips of up to 20 seconds and full-track transcription are separate options. Only enabling full-track transcription authorizes sending a full track. Provider charges, retention and deletion depend on your provider and account. Keys stay in the device keychain; removing a key in Settings prevents further requests with it.

## Your choices and questions

You can play, import and edit music without enabling AI. Melodies has no developer-hosted library or account. To ask about privacy or request deletion of support correspondence, email [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn).

# Melodies 隐私政策

## 2026 年 11 月 23 日生效

Melodies 由 Dev Go 提供。播放与曲库管理支持离线使用，无需注册账户。应用不使用广告或跟踪 SDK。

## 设备上的资料

导入的音频、封面、歌词、歌单、播放记录和设置保存在设备上。你可在应用中删除歌曲和封面。主动启用耳机控制时，动作数据仅在本地处理。

## 主动发起的传输

附近传歌将选中的内容直接发给你选择的设备。浏览器上传需要在手机上确认，通过局域网传输。选择微信等第三方应用分享文件时，接收应用按其隐私政策处理文件。

## 可选 AI 功能

配置服务并发起识别后，所选的 OpenAI、Kimi 或 DeepSeek 会收到文件名、歌名、歌手、专辑、时长、歌词摘录和你填写的提示，使用你自己的 API Key 处理。生成封面时会向 OpenAI 发送曲目信息。最多 20 秒的片段辅助和整首听写有独立开关，只有开启整首听写才授权发送完整曲目。服务商的计费、留存与删除取决于服务和账户设置。密钥保存在设备钥匙串，清空并保存后将停止使用该密钥。

## 你的选择和联系方式

不启用 AI 也可以播放、导入和编辑音乐。Melodies 不提供开发者托管的云端曲库或账户。隐私问题或删除支持邮件的请求可发送到 [lm@usev.cn](mailto:lm@usev.cn)。
